Idun is the open-source production wrapper for LangGraph and Google ADK agents. `pip install idun-agent-engine`, point it at your agent, and get a self-hosted FastAPI service with chat UI, admin panel, traces viewer, guardrails, memory, and MCP server support, all on your infrastructure.

## What you get

- **Framework adapters** for LangGraph and Google ADK, served with AG-UI streaming.
- **Guardrails** with 15+ built-in guards powered by Guardrails AI.
- **Observability** through Langfuse, Phoenix, LangSmith, or GCP, plus a local trace store with a waterfall viewer at `/admin/traces/`.
- **Memory** with in-memory, SQLite, or PostgreSQL checkpointers.
- **MCP tools** over stdio, SSE, streamable HTTP, or WebSocket with auto-discovery.
- **Prompts** with versioning and Jinja2 variables.
- **Integrations** for Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, and WhatsApp.
- **Auth**: OIDC SSO on `/agent/*` routes; `none` / `password` for the admin panel.
